Graduate student in mathematics, working on portfolio optimization. Currently surveying the literature in preparation for my thesis proposal, with the goal of producing publishable research on robust and distributionally robust portfolio selection.

It all starts with Markowitz’s mean-variance framework:

$$\min_{w}\ w^{\top}\Sigma w \quad \text{s.t.}\quad w^{\top}\mu \geq r_{\text{target}},\ \mathbf{1}^{\top}w = 1,\ w \geq 0$$

Research Interests

  • Robust optimization: robust portfolio selection under worst-case uncertainty sets
  • Distributionally robust optimization (DRO): expectation objectives over Wasserstein ambiguity sets
  • Parameter uncertainty: estimation-error sensitivity and regularization methods
